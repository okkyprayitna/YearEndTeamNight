Evening Schedule

Timeline of the Night

From sunset toasts to the final shutter of the photo booth, here is what we have prepared for you.

6:00 PM

Welcome Drinks & Sunset Reception

Arrive at Level 12 as dusk settles over the city skyline. Enjoy handcrafted welcome champagne, seasonal mocktails, artisanal canapés, and live acoustic lounge rhythms while catching up with colleagues across all teams.

6:45 PM

Dinner Feast & Live Stations

A gourmet buffet banquet featuring prime carving stations, grilled sea delicacies, organic plant-based creations, and warm dessert towers. Seating is open across heated terrace lounges and indoor dining suites.

7:45 PM

Annual Awards & Team Tributes

Honoring the extraordinary contributors, unsung champions, peer-voted superstars, and tenure milestones that shaped our past twelve months. Join us as leadership raises a special toast to everyone's collective dedication.

8:30 PM

Rooftop Games & High-Stakes Trivia

Get ready for high-energy cross-department trivia, rapid-fire team challenges, and our much-anticipated grand year-end raffle drawing featuring incredible tech, travel, and experience vouchers.

9:30 PM

Photo Booth & Starlight Social

Step into our 360° slow-motion video station and classic instant print photo booth equipped with glamorous golden props. Mingle under the stars, grab your commemorative gifts, and wrap up an unforgettable evening.

Guest Guidelines

Dress Code & What to Bring

Three essential tips to ensure you are comfortable, stylish, and fully prepared for rooftop festivities.

01

Smart Casual, with a Touch of Gold

Elevate your favorite smart casual attire with a festive golden sparkle. Whether it is a subtle gold tie, cufflinks, statement jewelry, a gold-threaded blazer, or sparkling shoes—bring your personal gleam to the celebration.

Polished, stylish & celebratory
02

Bring a Layer for Rooftop Breeze

Level 12 is equipped with state-of-the-art glass wind deflectors and overhead gas heaters, but open rooftop air can grow breezy after 8:00 PM. We strongly recommend bringing a light coat, stylish blazer, or warm shawl.

Heated lounges + jacket recommended
03

Office Keycard & Celebration Energy

Bring your physical employee access card or digital office badge to scan through the Level 12 express elevator gates seamlessly. Leave all work laptops behind, and just bring your appetite, festive spirit, and good vibes!

Express elevator entry at Lobby
Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question about attendance, logistics, or food? Click any question below to view the details.

Due to the rooftop's strict capacity regulations and fire codes, this event is exclusively reserved for current team members, interns, and direct contractors. This ensures all colleagues have ample space to celebrate comfortably and connect across departments!

Our catering partners are providing comprehensive gourmet options including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal-certified, and nut-free selections. All food stations will have clear allergy labeling. The bar will offer a curated range of red/white wines, craft beer, sparkling champagne, and zero-proof artisanal mocktails.

Enter through the main office tower lobby and proceed to the high-rise elevator bank (Lifts 5–8) marked for Level 12 Rooftop Access. Complimentary underground visitor parking is available starting from 5:00 PM—simply scan your parking token with the event reception host upon check-in.

Rain or shine, the party goes on! Level 12 features a high-grade motorized weatherproof glass canopy that fully encloses the dining and lounge space in under two minutes, along with climate-controlled indoor sky suites adjacent to the terrace. You will stay completely warm and dry regardless of the weather.
